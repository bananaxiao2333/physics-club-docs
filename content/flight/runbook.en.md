# Run of Show

<p class="memorial-kicker">THE RUNBOOK · FROM PREPARATION TO WRAP-UP</p>

## Preparation period (3 weeks to 1 day before the event)

!!! note "About the checkboxes"
    The preparation period is a to-do schedule in the original document, so this site presents it as a checklist.
    **Every box is empty** — the original never moved past the planning stage, and no item records having been carried out.

### 3 weeks before

- [ ] The overall lead finalises the final event plan and the overall budget, and completes school approval and the venue / equipment applications
- [ ] The coordination group compiles the full-process event document, clarifies the division of labour between the groups, and collects competition registration information

### 2 weeks before

- [ ] The logistics group gathers material requirements, compiles the purchasing list and gets it approved
- [ ] The publicity and filming group produces the warm-up poster, the folding diagrams, the check-in wall and the warm-up post, and publishes them through the school's channels
- [ ] The competition operations and design group draws up the competition rules / scoring criteria, sets up the registration channel and answers competitors' questions

### 1 week before (Monday)

- [ ] The overall lead completes the invitations and liaison for **3 principals** and the participating teachers
- [ ] The competition operations and design group collects registration information, completes the grouping of competitors and produces the schedule / score sheet / competitor numbers
- [ ] The competition operations and design group runs pre-duty training for referees, distance measurers and recorders
- [ ] The logistics group completes all material purchasing, sets up a ledger and keeps everything classified

### 1 day before

- [ ] The logistics group + the on-site execution group complete the zoning and marking of the venue and test equipment such as the laser rangefinder
- [ ] The overall lead + the vice-president organise all staff for a full run-through, simulating how emergencies would be handled
- [ ] Each group holds an internal meeting to reconfirm the next day's post duties and requirements
- [ ] The logistics group distributes all materials to their places and completes the final count

## Event day · Morning session (indoor gymnasium 08:00–12:30)

### 08:00–08:40 All staff on post and ready

1. Each group leader confirms that staff are on post, materials are in place and equipment is working
2. Key equipment and materials such as the laser rangefinder and the check-in wall are tested a second time
3. The emergency arrangements for each post are reconfirmed

### 09:00–10:30 Folding and free flight for everyone

1. The on-site execution group opens the signature and check-in area and guides students and teachers to sign, take photos and stick up wish notes
2. **胡益成、肖子扬** are on post at the teaching corner, teaching **6** paper plane folding patterns hand over hand and handing out the diagrams
3. The on-site execution group assigns posts to keep order in the free flight area, the distance challenge area and the creative display area
4. For students who reach the standard in the distance challenge area, the logistics group hands out the achievement award on the spot
5. The publicity and filming group films throughout, editing short videos in real time and posting them to the school's channels
6. The logistics group patrols the whole site, topping up A4 paper, prizes, stationery and other materials in good time
7. The logistics group deals quickly with temporary equipment failures on site
8. The overall lead + the vice-president patrol the whole site, coordinating emergencies and receiving the guests who arrive
9. The overall lead + the vice-president get students and teachers interacting and set the mood of the event

### 10:30–11:30 Engineering group showcase and testing

1. The competition operations and design group: 1 person hosts, 2 people take charge of judging + distance measurement, 1 person records and posts the results
2. 1 person in the competition operations and design group hands out prizes and 3 people keep order on site
3. The on-site execution group: 3 people guide students to watch in an orderly way and explain the test rules at the same time
4. The publicity and filming group films the highlights of the testing, records each team's results and prepares material for the afternoon's opening video

### 11:30–12:00 School-wide prediction contest

1. The on-site execution group hands out and collects the prediction tickets
2. The on-site execution group seals the collected prediction tickets in the ballot box
3. The coordination group posts the prediction draw rules and the draw time on site
4. The coordination group guides students to take part in the prediction in an orderly way and answers their questions

### 12:00–12:30 Morning wrap-up + short debrief

1. The logistics group collects the materials from each area, counts the remaining supplies and tidies the venue briefly
2. The competition operations and design group sorts out the morning's test results and confirms that the afternoon's competitors will be present
3. The publicity and filming group sorts the morning's footage and finishes editing the afternoon opening recap video
4. The overall lead + the vice-president gather the core team for a short **10-minute** debrief
5. In response to the morning's problems, the afternoon's arrangements and staffing are adjusted immediately

## Event day · Afternoon session (main sports field 13:30–16:00)

### 12:30–14:00 Final preparation before the competition

1. All staff are on post, and each group leader confirms materials, equipment and the state of the venue
2. The on-site execution group finishes setting up the isolation tape in the flight area on the sports field, dividing it into **two flight fields**

### 14:20–15:20 Official flight competition

1. **Group stage**: each competitor makes 1 preliminary flight + **3** official flights; the best result selects the **top 8**
2. **Final for the top 8**: the top 8 fly **5** times; combined with their group-stage results, the best score decides the champion, the runner-up and third place as well as the **school record holder**
3. **Student–teacher challenge**: student groups approach teachers themselves to arrange a contest; teachers may use the club's equipment, and **prizes are given win or lose**
4. **sink** is specially invited to serve as **chief judge**, responsible for interpreting the rules and making the final ruling on disputes
5. The competition operations and design group: 1 person hosts, keeping the pace of the competition and announcing competitors' information and results
6. 2 people in the competition operations and design group check competitors in, and 3 people act as flight judges supervising the flight process
7. 3 people in the competition operations and design group handle laser ranging, recording flight data precisely
8. 2 people in the competition operations and design group score and **post the results live by projection**, ensuring openness and transparency
9. 2 people in the competition operations and design group keep order in the competition area and stop unrelated people from entering the flight area
10. The publicity and filming group films the highlights of the competition throughout and records material for the awards
11. The overall lead + the vice-president keep the pace of the whole event, receive the judges and guests and handle sudden disputes

!!! note "There is a second version of the competition format"
    [Flight festival role assignments](../about/sources/index.md#flight-roles "飞行节分工安排 2.docx") records **1 preliminary flight + 2 official flights**, and **3** flights in the final for the top 8.
    The two original documents disagree; they are kept side by side here and not merged.
    See [Sources](../about/sources/index.md).

### 15:20–15:40 Awards ceremony + announcement of the school record

1. The coordination group receives the awards guests (principals / teachers) and guides them onto the stage
2. The coordination group keeps to the order and flow of the awards, making sure the ceremony runs smoothly
3. The competition operations and design group announces this year's **school paper plane flight record** and posts the final list of winners
4. The competition operations and design group lays out certificates, medals and prizes by award category to support the ceremony
5. The on-site execution group completes the prediction lucky draw and checks the list of winning students
6. The on-site execution group guides the winning students to collect their prizes in order and answers questions about collecting them
7. The publicity and filming group films the awards throughout, recording the highlights

### 15:40–16:00 Afternoon wrap-up

1. All staff clear the venue together, returning tables and chairs to their places and taking the rubbish away
2. The logistics group counts all materials and equipment, checking quantities and keeping a record
3. The logistics group immediately returns everything borrowed from the school
4. The overall lead + the vice-president gather all staff for a short summary meeting and thank them for their work

## Wrap-up and review period (within 1 week of the event)

### Within 24 hours of the event

1. The publicity and filming group finishes and publishes the event recap post and the short video
2. The publicity and filming group sorts all the footage and files it by category
3. The publicity and filming group submits a review report on the event's reach data

### Within 3 days of the event

1. The competition operations and design group posts the final list of winners and the school record within the school
2. The competition operations and design group finishes making and putting up the school record display board
3. The logistics group completes the stocktake of the remaining materials into storage and keeps a ledger record
4. The logistics group checks how the funds were used and submits the final financial statement and reimbursement material

## Core awards

### Morning session · Achievement award

**No quota.** Students who reach any of the **10 m / 20 m / 30 m** challenge lines in the distance challenge area collect the corresponding prize immediately.

### Afternoon session · Official awards

| Award | Places | How it is decided |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Competition Core Award · Champion | 1 | By distance flown in the official competition |
| Competition Core Award · Runner-up | 2 | By distance flown in the official competition |
| Competition Core Award · Third place | 3 | By distance flown in the official competition |
| School record holder | 1 | The longest flight of this event |
| Best Creative Paper Plane Award | First 1 / Second 3 / Third 5 | School-wide vote + judges |
| Student–Teacher Interaction Popularity Award | 3 | The student group with the most interaction and the highest popularity in the student–teacher challenge |
| Prediction Lucky Prize | **30** | Drawn at random from the students who predicted correctly |
| Outstanding Volunteer Award | Several | Selected from the staff who stood out |

!!! note "The prediction lucky prize has two versions for its number"
    The run of show document says **30**, the division-of-labour document says **unlimited**. They are kept side by side here.

Groups of **at most six**; prizes are awarded by group.
