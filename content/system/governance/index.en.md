# Governance & Roster

<p class="memorial-kicker">GOVERNANCE · RULES, OFFICERS & ROSTERS</p>

A club needs more than activities; it also needs rules and a division of labour. This part gathers two things:
**how money is collected, spent and recorded**, and **who holds which position and belongs to which group**.

Both pages take the originals in the `资料/` directory as their authority:

- [Membership Dues and Contribution Credits](fees.md) — the club dues and contribution-credit rules set out in full at the 27 April 2026 assembly
- [Organisational Structure and Roster](roster.md) — 14 officers, four groups, and the list of advisors

!!! warning "A note on conventions"
    The original CSVs and slide decks are in traditional Chinese characters; this site gives the whole page in the conventional simplified forms, quotations and personal and group names included.
    Where the pages find contradictions in the material (dates, amounts, headcounts), they **keep the versions side by side** and neither merge them nor draw inferences.
