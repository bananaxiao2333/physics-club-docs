# Rules & Merit

<p class="memorial-kicker">GOVERNANCE &amp; THE MERIT SYSTEM</p>

## A club needs rules, not only events

Beyond its events, the club also left behind two kinds of **organisational** material:
a set of rules about money and people, and a mechanism for recording participation
and contribution.

This part gathers those two things:

| Section | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Governance & Rosters](governance/index.md) | The dues and contribution-points system, the structure and the rosters |
| [The Merit System](merit/index.md) | The late-period internal mechanism for dues, contribution and rewards |

## On sourcing

Both pages are based on the original files in the `资料/` directory and **use no
second-hand retelling**.

The merit system needs a word of explanation: it was an **organisational experiment**.
The surviving material includes the full rules, the record model and a glossary, and
also parts that never landed. This site preserves that material as it stands, keeps its
boundary notes, and does not decide on the reader's behalf which parts were carried out.

!!! warning "On rosters and records"
    Rosters and ledgers are **primary data**: names, amounts and dates are transcribed
    from the originals, without alteration or summary. For how personal information was
    weighed, see [Thanks · Editorial notes](../about/thanks/index.md).
