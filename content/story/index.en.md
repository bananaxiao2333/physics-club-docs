# The Club's Story

<p class="memorial-kicker">01 / THE MEMORY COLLECTION · OUR STORY</p>

## Curiosity came first, and then our story

Not just a name on a list of activities.
It was a place where **understanding, expression and practice** could happen.

## The club's original intent: approaching physics in our own way

Material from the assembly of 21 April 2026 describes the club this way: it was founded to give students a platform to **understand physics, express physics and practise physics**.

The opening script divided the club's direction into two lines:

1. **Make physics interesting, tangible and open to participation** — experiments, activities, challenges;
2. **Let students who want to go deeper go further** — competition problem walkthroughs and thinking training.

Experiments, project design, photography, editing, copywriting and event organising could all be ways of taking part.
