<p class="memorial-kicker">06 / THE MEMORY COLLECTION · THE ASSEMBLY ROOM</p>

## Turning back to that page

Three assembly slide decks from spring 2026. **31 pages of content**, kept in their original order.

!!! info "On how the content is treated"
    The pages below all **keep the original decks as they were**, including the ideas in them that were never carried out.
    The recruitment, dues and system designs described in these plans are treated as **historical material**; this site offers no sign-up, payment or club account services.

=== "04.21 · Assembly and recruitment"

    **12 pages** — [Download the page-by-page PDF](../../assets/originals/assembly-421.pdf){ target=_blank }
    **This PDF is compiled by this site** from the 12 page images below — it is not the original export: the surviving PDF export is truncated and cannot be opened.

    The running order was fixed: badges → paper plane activity → competition results → recruitment → social media department → membership dues.

    01. Assembly cover

    03. Badges presented to the officers

    07. About recruitment

    10. Competition results
    11. Q&A
    12. Thank you for listening
