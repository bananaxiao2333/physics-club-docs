04.27 Assembly · Page-by-page record¶

SLIDES 1–14 · 2026.04.27

What this page is

This page transcribes all 14 pages of 27 April assembly deck, page by page. The original is in Traditional Chinese and quotations are transcribed into simplified characters. The material between slides is added by this site, and the English renderings are added by this site as well.

The deck has two subjects: changes to the paper plane challenge (slides 1–3) and the membership dues and contribution credit system (slides 6–14).

Slide 1 · 三项变更 (Three changes)¶

紙飛機挑戰賽活動說明

  1. 時間變動：改為 15.20–16.20
  2. 報名通道更改
  3. 現場佈局及流程介紹

English rendering.

Paper plane challenge — activity notes.

  1. Time change: moved to 15.20–16.20
  2. Registration channel changed
  3. On-site layout and run-through

This is the third version of the times

The 4.21 final version says 14:00–16:30; this slide changes it to 15:20–16:20; the poster draft says 15:00–16:10; the run-of-show document schedules the activity across a whole day.

The title of this slide uses the word “變動” (change), which makes this an active adjustment rather than a new plan.

Slide 2 · 場地地圖 (Venue map)¶

紙飛機挑戰賽場地圖

English rendering.

Paper plane challenge venue map.

The venue map marks four surrounding buildings and three competition areas:

Category Labels
Surrounding buildings Canteen · Gymnasium · Teaching building · Dormitory
Competition areas Preparation area · Flight area · Start point

測量須知 以起點和紙飛機落點之間的直線距離為最終成績

English rendering.

How the measurement is done: the final result is the straight-line distance between the start point and where the paper plane lands.

How the measure of a result was settled

This is the clearest definition of how results were measured anywhere in the surviving material. The same measure is echoed in Flight festival promotion script (“precise recording with laser ranging equipment”) and in the run-of-show document (a laser ranging post); together the three explain why a laser rangefinder had to be bought rather than using a tape measure.

The vector original of the venue map is in Competition venue map.

Slide 3 · 疊法展示 (Folding demonstration)¶

紙飛機疊法展示 現場将会有演示提供

English rendering.

Paper plane folding demonstration. A demonstration will be provided on site.

Where the “six folding methods” come from

This slide says only that “a demonstration will be provided” and gives no number. The figure of “6 種紙飛機折法” (six paper plane folding methods) comes from Flight festival promotion script and from A4 Flight Festival runbook (the teaching specialist post). This slide can be read as a preparatory note for that arrangement.

Slide 4 · 自媒體部門競選流程說明 (How the social media department election runs)¶

自媒體部門競選流程說明

我們更看重的是：你是否願意認真做一件事，是否願意在團隊裏承擔責任， 是否願意把自己的興趣，變成真正的能力。

讓熱愛被看見 · 讓內容產生價值 · 讓社團擁有自己的未來

English rendering.

How the social media department election runs.

What we value more is: whether you are willing to do something seriously, whether you are willing to take on responsibilities within the team, and whether you are willing to turn your interests into real abilities.

Let what we love be seen · let the content have value · let the club have a future of its own.

From “notes” to “election”

The 4.21 assembly was the social media department's founding statement; the title of this slide has become “競選流程說明” (notes on the election process), which shows that the department had entered actual recruitment.

The wording on this slide is exactly the same as the English paragraph on slide 9 of 4.21, but the four items — recruiting areas / how it runs / what the department aims at / where it applies — are gone, leaving only the statement of values.

Slide 5 · 積分系統簡介 (Introduction to the points system)¶

物理社積分系統简介

物理社積分系統記錄成員參與實驗、活動、分享和競賽的表現， 積分可兌換獎勵，激勵大家積極探索物理奧秘。

English rendering.

Introduction to the club's points system.

The club's points system records how members perform in experiments, activities, sharing sessions and competitions; points can be exchanged for rewards, encouraging everyone to explore the mysteries of physics.

Feature Notes
Self-service Supports self-managed accounts
Always on Open for login 7×24

The wording of this slide conflicts with slide 9

This slide says credits “可兌換獎勵” (can be exchanged for rewards), while slide 9, “The position of contribution credits”, explicitly lists what credits should not be taken for.

The two slides are four pages apart and their force is entirely different. This site keeps them side by side and does not judge which page represents the final position. See The merit system.

Slide 6 · 制度說明首頁 (Opening page of the system)¶

物理社社費與貢獻積分制度說明 社費 · 貢獻積分 · 公開監督 · 合規邊界

English rendering.

The club's membership dues and contribution credit system. Dues · contribution credits · public oversight · compliance boundaries.

核心立場 - 讓社團活動更穩定 - 讓資金和規則更透明 - 讓制度邊界更清晰

English rendering.

Core position

  • Make club activities more stable
  • Make funding and rules more transparent
  • Make the boundaries of the system clearer

Four principles:

Principle Notes
Public and transparent Income sources, uses of spending, receipts and balance all open to inspection
Voluntary participation Paying dues is not a precondition for joining the club
Compliant operation Dues and credits stay within bounds; no design that turns them into currency
Club self-governance Supervised by the club's own treasurer and cashier, with the rules published in advance

依據：社費草案、法律條文與定義、穩定／代幣風險建議文檔

English rendering.

Basis: the dues draft, the legal provisions and definitions, and the document on stablecoin／token risk.

The three documents listed under “basis”

This slide names the three documents the system's design rests on: the dues draft, the legal provisions and definitions, and the document advising on stablecoin／token risk.

These are Merit rules draft, Legal provisions and definitions and Stability · discussion notes in 资料/. In line with the scope published in White papers and the retention boundary, this site does not publish their contents.

Slide 7 · 為什麼需要這套制度 (Why this system is needed)¶

為什麼需要這套制度

制度不是為了「收費」，而是為了解決社團活動運行中的現實問題。

English rendering.

Why this system is needed.

The system is not there to “charge money”, but to solve the practical problems in running club activities.

# Problem How it shows up
1 The gap in reimbursement Formal approval and reimbursement at the school take time; equipment and materials often have to be bought in advance; the club needs the ability to cover costs for a short while
2 Varied activity spending Experiments, competitions, regular meetings and welfare activities all cost money; some spending may fall outside unified reimbursement; the purpose of each sum has to be kept clear
3 Trust and rules If the rules for dues and credits are unclear they invite misunderstanding; opaque accounts cause a crisis of trust; setting the rules in advance reduces disputes

用制度化方式保障活動持續開展，而不是用臨時辦法解決每一次支出。

English rendering.

The aim is to keep activities going by institutional means, not to solve every payment with an ad hoc measure.

How this page argues its case

This slide argues for the system with one structure: “three practical problems → one conclusion by contrast”. The most concrete item is “the gap in reimbursement / the ability to cover costs for a short while”: it explains why the club needed to hold cash, not merely keep accounts.

Slide 8 · 社費資金池設計 (Design of the dues fund pools)¶

社費資金池設計 將經費用途分清楚，是後續公開監督和風險控制的基礎。

English rendering.

Design of the dues fund pools. Keeping the uses of the funds clearly separated is the basis for later public oversight and risk control.

Fixed fund pool

  • Used to cover reimbursable club spending in the meantime
  • Helps bridge the time gap before the school approves
  • Emphasis on keeping receipts and on later settlement
  • Should not be designed as a source of personal income

Activity fund pool

  • Used to cover part of the non-reimbursable activity costs
  • Such as small materials, organising activities and welfare arrangements
  • Spending should be directly related to club activities
  • Amounts, uses and the approval process must be public

建議原則：社費自願、用途明確、憑證齊全、定期公示。

English rendering.

Proposed principles: dues voluntary, uses clear, receipts complete, published regularly.

The naming difference between “活動資金池” and “流動資金池”

The second pool on this slide is called “活動資金池” (activity fund pool), while 物理社功勋系统资金流转白皮书 and 会员公开白皮书 call it “流動資金池” (circulating fund pool), defined there as “the part that enters a member's personal account and can be used for activities and resources inside the club”.

The two describe similar duties without being identical: this slide stresses “non-reimbursable costs”, the white papers stress “credited to the personal account”. Both are kept side by side.

Slide 9 · 貢獻積分的定位 (The position of contribution credits)¶

貢獻積分的定位 積分可以記錄貢獻，但絕不能被包裝成貨幣、投資或收益憑證。

English rendering.

The position of contribution credits. Credits can record contribution, but must never be packaged as currency, an investment or a certificate of return.

What it should be What it should not be
✓ A record of contribution inside the club ✕ Currency, a token or a coin
✓ A tool for encouraging participation ✕ An investment or financing instrument
✓ A record of entitlement to welfare activities ✕ A promise of fixed returns or dividends
✓ A supporting mechanism for club self-governance ✕ A certificate that can be transferred, traded or cashed out

This slide sets the compliance floor of the whole system

The wording of the four items on the right (“must never be packaged as”) is the strongest language in the whole deck. It separates credits from three things — currency, investment, certificates of return — and corresponds to the analysis of the “four characteristics” of token financing and illegal fundraising in Legal provisions and definitions.

Slide 10 · 積分如何獲得與使用 (How credits are earned and used)¶

積分如何獲得與使用 積分應圍繞真實貢獻產生，規則必須提前公佈。

English rendering.

How credits are earned and used. Credits should arise from real contribution, and the rules must be published in advance.

Source Examples
Participation Attending regular meetings, taking part in experiments, joining club activities
Contribution Explaining problems, helping to organise, completing club tasks
Performance Winning a competition, standing out at a large event
Record Forming contribution credits under published rules

使用邊界 - 僅限社團內部福利活動或權益記錄 - 不得轉讓、交易、折現或承諾經濟回報 - 不得用積分製造「投入越多收益越高」的預期

English rendering.

Boundaries on use

  • Confined to welfare activities inside the club, or to records of entitlement
  • May not be transferred, traded, cashed out or promised as a financial return
  • May not be used to create the expectation that “the more you put in, the higher the return”

“不得製造預期” (may not create expectations)

The third boundary is the most unusual on this slide: what it restricts is not an act but the formation of an expectation. Logically it complements slide 9's “not an investment instrument”.

Slide 11 · 爭議處理機制 (How disputes are handled)¶

爭議處理機制 規則越清晰，越能保護社員、社團幹部和指導老師。

English rendering.

How disputes are handled. The clearer the rules, the better they protect members, club officers and the supervising teacher.

Four steps:

Step Action
1 Submit an application The member applies in writing and gives the reason
2 Review and record Review under the rules, keeping the date, the amount and the basis
3 Report the outcome Tell the member the outcome, and publish the rule relied on if necessary
4 Appeal and explain Members may object, add further explanation or ask for a review

Damage to equipment

  • Should be handled on the basis of facts, evidence and the clauses of the constitution
  • Simply deducting credits is not recommended as a substitute for formal compensation
  • Room should be left for explanation, negotiation and appeal

Avoiding overbearing clauses

  • Reduce one-sided statements such as “the club holds the final right of interpretation”
  • Replace them with “interpretation according to the constitution, open to oversight and appeal”
  • Outcomes should be traceable and explainable

One point on this slide worth singling out

The section on avoiding overbearing clauses actively limits the club's own power, and asks for “interpretation according to the constitution, open to oversight and appeal” in place of “the club holds the final right of interpretation”. That is not common in the governing documents of student clubs.

Slide 12 · 公開帳本與知情權 (The public ledger and the right to know)¶

公開帳本與知情權 透明不是負擔，而是減少誤會和保護社團幹部的機制。

English rendering.

The public ledger and the right to know. Transparency is not a burden but a mechanism that reduces misunderstanding and protects club officers.

Layer Content
The public ledger Receipts · flows · balance
Overall income and spending Income sources published; uses of spending published; summarised and published regularly
Personal records One's own dues record; one's own credit record
Original receipts Screenshots of payment; invoices or receipts; purchasing lists
Oversight mechanisms Members may ask; teachers advise; problems may be appealed

Transparency argued as a protection for officers

The subtitle defines transparency as a mechanism that protects club officers, not merely one that restrains them. That view differs from the usual “transparency is for oversight”.

Slide 13 · 執行落地流程 (Putting the system into practice)¶

執行落地流程 建議先試行、再修訂，避免制度一開始就過重或過急。

English rendering.

Putting the system into practice. The suggestion is to try it first and revise afterwards, so that the system is not too heavy or too hasty from the start.

Step Action
1 Publish the draft Explain the purpose of the system to members and teachers and gather opinions in one place
2 Revise the detailed rules Make the rules for dues, credits, oversight and dispute handling explicit
3 Set up the ledger Record income and spending, receipts, personal records and the publication cycle in one place
4 Review regularly Check how it works each month or each term and revise what does not

“先試行、再修訂” (try it first, revise afterwards)

This slide makes clear that the system was not written once and left as it was, but that the mechanism of revision is built into the process itself (step 4). That matches the later multi-version evolution of the merit system — v1.0 → the first official version → the member-public version.

Slide 14 · 收束 (Closing)¶

讓制度服務社團建設

每一筆錢都花在明處， 每一份貢獻都被公平記錄。

English rendering.

Let the system serve the building of the club.

Every sum of money is spent in the open, every contribution is recorded fairly.

The end answering the beginning

The two lines of the last page answer respectively to public and transparent among the four principles on slide 6 (money spent in the open) and to the position of credits on slides 9–10 (contribution recorded, but not turned into currency).
