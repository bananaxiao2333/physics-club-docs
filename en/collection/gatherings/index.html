Turning back to that page¶

Three assembly slide decks from spring 2026. 31 pages of content, kept in their original order.

On how the content is treated

The pages below all keep the original decks as they were, including the ideas in them that were never carried out. The recruitment, dues and system designs described in these plans are treated as historical material; this site offers no sign-up, payment or club account services.

Data & privacy

This site collects no visitor data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no cookie and no third-party request of any kind. Fonts, styles and scripts are all self-hosted, so any page contacts this site’s domain only. A single light/dark display preference is stored locally by the browser, and is removed when browser data is cleared.
