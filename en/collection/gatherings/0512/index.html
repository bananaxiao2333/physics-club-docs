05.12 Assembly · Page-by-page record¶

SLIDES 1–5 · 2026.05.12

What this page is

This page transcribes all 5 pages of 12 May assembly deck, page by page. The material between slides is added by this site, and the English renderings are added by this site as well.

This is the latest assembly file in the surviving material, and the only deck containing a complete group roster and the merit system.

Page order and hidden pages

This deck has 5 pages in all, but pages 1 and 4 contain no text at all, and the cover appears on page 3. So only 3 pages have content: page 2 (the group roster), page 3 (the cover), page 5 (the merit system).

In presentation order the roster on page 2 comes before the cover, which matches the note “a hidden page in the original” on the original site.

The four groups come to 85 people (22 + 19 + 23 + 21).

This is the largest roster in the surviving material. Fourteen of the officers also appear in the badge list on slide 3 of the 04.21 assembly, which allows a cross-check.

How the group roster was produced

The original table uses merged cells, and flattening it would lose which column a name belongs to. The roster on this page was entered cell by cell, by parsing the table XML of the deck and following cell coordinates, rather than transcribed linearly in reading order.

The group colours (#910000 / #3b229f / #887300 / #6b0089) are in Member group roster. The full roster is in Governance and roster.

Slide 5 · 功勳系統 (The merit system)¶

ASJ英東物理社功勋系统

English rendering.

ASJ Yingdong Physics Club merit system.

What this slide actually contains

This slide gives only a title; its content was a demonstration (a video or a live showing). The merit system demonstration video kept in Merit system demo video is very probably the material used in that slot.

The full text of the merit system is in The merit system, The fund flow model and White papers and the retention boundary.

The standing of this deck¶

Dimension Notes
Date 2026.05.12, the latest in the surviving material
Length 5 pages, of which 3 have content
Found only here the only complete group roster (85 people); the only demonstration of the merit system
Missing no timetable, no activity plan, no breakdown of spending

The group roster on page 2 and the merit system on page 5 are the only two substantial pieces of content in this deck. Both point to the same thing: in May the club entered a stage of organisation — moving from “running activities” to “setting rules and dividing into groups”.
