04.21 Assembly and recruitment · Page-by-page record¶

SLIDES 1–12 · 2026.04.21 · FINAL

What this page is

This page transcribes all 12 pages of 21 April assembly deck (final), page by page. The deck is in Traditional Chinese; quotations are transcribed into simplified characters1, and the material between slides is added by this site. The English renderings are added by this site as well.

For the wider context see Assembly Scripts; for the minutes of the assembly see The first assembly and recruitment.

Slide 1 · 封面 (Cover)¶

ASJ英東物理社集會暨招新活動 ASJ Yingdong Physics Club Gathering and Recruitment Event 2026/04/21

This was the club's first formal assembly this term, and it also served as recruitment. The Chinese–English bilingual title continues on every later page, forming the fixed layout of this deck.

Slide 2 · 關於 ASJ 英東物理社團 (About the ASJ Yingdong Physics Club)¶

關於 / About

ASJ Physics Club 的誕生，並非只為了解題， 也不是為了增加課外活動的數量，而是希望創造一個 讓學生用自己的方式理解物理、表達物理、實踐物理的平臺。

English rendering.

About.

The ASJ Physics Club was born not only to solve problems, nor to add one more extracurricular activity, but to create a platform for students to understand physics, express physics and practise physics in their own way.

The standing of this page

This is the only place in all the surviving material where the club's purpose is defined directly, and the source of the later phrase “understand physics, express physics, practise physics”. That phrase is quoted again and again in the opening script and in the recruitment speech.

The three-part sentence “not… nor… but…” explicitly rules out two things the club was not: problem-solving drills and the number of extracurricular activities.

Slide 5 · 會費收集說明 (Membership dues collection)¶

會費收集說明 / Membership Fee Collection Instructions

為了更好地支持本學期及以後的物理社團活動的開展， 本學期將設置社團會費自願繳納機制。

特別說明的是：本次會費繳納完全遵循自願原則。 繳納會費並不是加入社團的前提，不繳納也不會影響同學參與社團日常活動。

In order to better support the development of the physics club activities this term, a voluntary membership fee payment system will be implemented this term.

It should be noted specifically: This fee payment is entirely based on a voluntary principle. Paying the fee is not a prerequisite for joining the club; failure to pay will not affect students' participation in the club's daily activities.

The voluntary principle stated three times

This slide defines the same thing three times over — “自願繳納機制”, “完全遵循自願原則”, “不繳納也不會影響” — with the wording growing stronger each time. It is the most cautious page in the surviving material on the question of dues.

The specific uses and the principles for spending them are set out in Notes on how dues were used; the system design is in Membership dues and contribution credits.

Slide 6 · 關於科技節紙飛機說明 (About the paper planes at the Science and Technology Festival)¶

關於科技節紙飛機說明 / Explanation of Paper Airplanes for the Science and Technology Festival

以紙為帆，航跡英東

時間：4月30日 14：00 到 16:30

English rendering.

Explanation of Paper Airplanes for the Science and Technology Festival.

With paper as the sail, the journey reaches as far as the east.

Time: 30 April, 14:00 to 16:30.

With paper as the sail, the journey reaches as far as the east.

This slide lists three slots:

Time Segment
14:00–15:15 Folding for everyone · free flight
14:30–15:30 School-wide guessing game
15:30–17:00 The official flight competition

Times conflict within this page and between pages

  • Within the page: the heading says 14:00–16:30, but the last slot runs to 17:00, half an hour later than the heading;
  • Between pages: 27 April assembly deck moves the activity to 15:20–16:20; the poster draft says 15:00–16:10; the run-of-show document schedules it across a whole day.

All four versions are kept side by side; this site does not infer which time was finally used. For the full comparison see Sources · conflicting statements of time.

Slide 7 · 關於招新 (About recruitment)¶

關於招新 / Regarding the recruitment of new members

新學期，物理社團正式開啟招新。如果你對物理有興趣，如果你喜歡思考、喜歡探索、 喜歡動手，如果你曾經被某一個實驗、某一個現象、某一個問題點燃過好奇心， 那麼歡迎你加入我們。

你不需要一開始就「很厲害」，也不需要已經學會很多超綱知識。 真正重要的是，你願不願意保持好奇，願不願意去問一個「為什麼」， 願不願意在看似普通的世界裏，發現那些不普通的規律。

English rendering.

Regarding the recruitment of new members.

In the new term, the physics club formally opens recruitment. If you are interested in physics, if you like to think, like to explore, like to work with your hands, if some experiment, some phenomenon, some question has ever caught your curiosity, then you are welcome to join us.

You do not have to be “very good” from the start, and you do not need to have learned a great deal beyond the syllabus. What really matters is whether you are willing to stay curious, willing to ask “why”, willing to find the uncommon laws in a world that looks ordinary.

How the threshold for recruitment is put

This slide explicitly removes the knowledge threshold (“you do not have to be very good”, “no knowledge beyond the syllabus”), and turns the test into a matter of willingness (curiosity / asking further / noticing). The wording answers directly to the club's purpose on slide 2.

Slide 8 · 活動照片 (Event photographs)¶

活動照片

物理不是少數人的舞臺。它屬於願意認真思考、願意反復推敲、願意把問題真正想透的人。

2026 春季學期，讓我們帶著清晰的目標與更高的標準，再次出發。期待今年，我們再創佳績。

歡迎 ASJ英東物理社招新內容

來自「雞蛋撞地球」

English rendering.

Event photographs.

Physics is not a stage for a chosen few. It belongs to those who are willing to think carefully, to weigh a question over and over, and to think it through to the end.

In the spring term of 2026, let us set out again with clear goals and a higher standard. We look forward to doing well again this year.

Welcome ASJ Yingdong Physics Club recruitment material

From “Egg Drop”.

What this slide actually contains

This slide uses photographs of “雞蛋撞地球” (“Egg Drop”) as its recruitment image, captioned “Physics is not a stage for a chosen few”.

“Egg Drop” is the only activity in the surviving material with no plan and no date of its own; it is mentioned only on this slide and in Recruitment talk script. See Egg Drop.

Slide 9 · 自媒體部門說明 (The social media department)¶

招募方向 拍攝、剪輯、策劃、文案、封面設計、運營

運轉方式 盈利前由 Sink 每月注入 1000 元專項經費

部門目標 讓熱愛被看見，讓內容產生價值，讓社團擁有自己的未來

適用場景 公眾號推文 / 招新發佈 / 集會宣講配套文案

讓熱愛被看見 · 讓內容產生價值 · 讓社團擁有自己的未來

English rendering.

What we are looking for — filming, editing, planning, copywriting, cover design, operations.

How it runs — before it makes a profit, Sink puts in 1000 yuan a month as dedicated funding.

What the department aims at — let what we love be seen, let the content have value, let the club have a future of its own.

Where it applies — the club's WeChat account posts / recruitment announcements / supporting copy for assembly talks.

Let what we love be seen · let the content have value · let the club have a future of its own.

What we value more is: whether you are willing to do something seriously, whether you are willing to take on responsibilities within the team, and whether you are willing to turn your interests into real abilities.

What this slide is

This is the social media department's founding statement, with four elements: what it recruits for, how it runs, what the department aims at, and where it applies. The line “before it turns a profit, Sink puts in a dedicated 1000 yuan a month” was announced publicly at the assembly, and is the only record of a funding source in the surviving material.

Slide 10 · 物理競賽成果展示 (Physics competition results)¶

物理競賽成果展示

獲獎率 100% 金獎得獎率（四捨五入）90%

English rendering.

Physics competition results.

Award rate 100%; gold award rate (rounded) 90%.

How this slide differs from the recruitment speech

Recruitment talk script says “85% won gold”; this final slide says “90% (rounded)”.

The two differ by 5 percentage points, and this site keeps both side by side. For the conclusion and the sources see Physics competition · 100% award rate.

This is the only slide in the deck built around numbers, carrying two figures and no embellishment.

Slide 11 · Q&A¶

Q&A

可以自由提問，關於會費、紙飛機活動、招新及競賽相關等皆可。 現場提問，現場回答。

English rendering.

Q&A.

You may ask anything you like — about membership dues, the paper plane activity, recruitment, the competition, and so on. Questions on the spot, answers on the spot.

The scope of the open questions

The four kinds of question listed on this slide — dues, the paper plane activity, recruitment, the competition — correspond one to one with the assembly's four main sections, which shows that a question slot was planned for from the start.

Opening assembly speaker's script contains no spoken script for the Q&A slot, which suggests the slot was answered off the cuff, with no text surviving.

Slide 12 · 收束 (Closing)¶

感謝聆聽 ASJ英東物理社，啟動！

Thank you for listening to the ASJ Yingdong Physics Club. Let's start!

“啟動”

The last page closes with “啟動” (launch) rather than “end”, matching the opening script's line “today is only the beginning”.
