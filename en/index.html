A PLACE TO ASK WHY

Beyond physics,
we were exploring freely.

ASJ Yingdong Physics Club once hoped to build a platform: one where students could understand physics, express physics and practice physics in their own way.

What is collected here are the scripts, activity plans, designs and photographs it left behind. The club no longer exists, but the work that was done in earnest is worth remembering.
