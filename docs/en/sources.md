## Editorial notes

### Photography and visual material

Photographs come from `微信assets`; the paper-plane images, venue map and poster
come from the same materials folder or were extracted from the decks.
Images are optimised for web viewing. **No stock photography is passed off as
club activity.**

### Conflicting timings

Four different versions of the flight-festival timing survive. This site
presents all of them rather than choosing:

| Source | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| 21 April final deck | 30 April, **14:00–16:30** |
| Division of labour | 30 April, **14:00–16:30** (sections running to 17:30) |
| 27 April assembly | changed to **15:20–16:20** |
| Original poster | **15:00–16:10** |
| Runbook | **Full day**: indoors 08:00–12:30 + field 13:30–16:00 |

### What cannot be confirmed

!!! warning "What this site does not supply"
    - **The club's end date and reason** — not confirmable from the materials.
    - **The flight festival's results** — only planning documents and blank
      forms survive; **there is no record of any result**.
    - **Exact competition dates** — the materials say only "last year".

### Language

The Chinese edition is the **authoritative and complete version** of this
memorial. This English section is a reading path: it covers the core record but
does not reproduce every page, including the full 31-slide transcription and
the complete 85-member roster.
