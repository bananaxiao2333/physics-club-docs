## The fee and contribution-points system

### Stated position

The system's stated purpose is **not to charge money** but to solve practical
problems in running the club:

| Problem | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Reimbursement lag** | School approval and reimbursement take time, while equipment often has to be bought in advance — the club needs short-term bridging capacity |
| **Varied spending** | Experiments, competitions, meetings and welfare activities all cost money, and some falls outside unified reimbursement — fund purposes must be clearly separated |
| **Trust and rules** | Unclear rules on fees and points breed misunderstanding; an opaque ledger causes a trust crisis; writing rules early reduces disputes |

### Four principles

| Principle | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Transparency** | Income sources, expenditure purposes, receipts and balances are all inspectable |
| **Voluntary participation** | Paying the fee is not a prerequisite for joining; not paying does not affect participation |
| **Compliance** | Fees and points do not cross boundaries; no monetisation design |
| **Club self-governance** | Supervised by an in-club treasurer and accountant; rules published in advance |

### Fund pools

**Fixed pool** — bridges reimbursable expenditure across the approval gap;
requires receipts and later settlement; **must not be designed as a source of
personal income.**

**Activity pool** — covers spending that cannot be reimbursed, such as small
materials, event organisation, and welfare arrangements; spending must be
directly related to club activity; amounts, purposes and approval flow are public.

> Proposed principles: **fees voluntary, purposes explicit, receipts complete,
> published periodically.**

### What points are — and are not

| They should be | They must not be |
| --- | --- |
| ✓ An internal contribution record | ✕ Currency, a token, or a pass |
| ✓ A tool for encouraging participation | ✕ An investment or financing instrument |
| ✓ A record of welfare entitlements | ✕ A promise of fixed return or dividend |
| ✓ An aid to club self-governance | ✕ A transferable, tradable or cashable certificate |

### Use boundaries

- Only for internal welfare activities or entitlement records
- **May not be transferred, traded, discounted, or used to promise economic return**
- **May not be used to create the expectation that more input yields more return**

### Dispute resolution

1. **Submit a request** — a member files a written request stating the reason
2. **Review and record** — reviewed per the rules, retaining time, amount and basis
3. **Return the outcome** — the decision is communicated; the rule basis is published where necessary
4. **Appeal** — members may raise objections, add explanation, or request review

!!! note "A clause worth singling out"
    The system explicitly moves away from
    "the club reserves final interpretation" toward
    "**interpreted per the charter, subject to oversight and appeal**",
    and requires decisions to be traceable and explainable.

    A student club voluntarily limiting its own authority is unusual.

### Public ledger

| Level | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Overall income and expenditure | Sources published; purposes published; summarised periodically |
| Personal records | The member's own fee and points records |
| Original receipts | Payment screenshots, invoices or receipts, purchase lists |
| Oversight | Member enquiries, teacher guidance, dispute appeals |
