## Slide 2 · 關於 ASJ 英東物理社團 (About the ASJ Yingdong Physics Club)

> **關於 / About**
>
> ASJ Physics Club 的誕生，並非只為了解題，
> 也不是為了增加課外活動的數量，而是希望創造一個
> **讓學生用自己的方式理解物理、表達物理、實踐物理**的平臺。

**English rendering.**

About.

The ASJ Physics Club was born not only to solve problems, nor to add one more extracurricular activity,
but to create **a platform for students to understand physics, express physics and practise physics in their own way.**

!!! note "The standing of this page"
    This is **the only place in all the surviving material where the club's purpose is defined directly**,
    and the source of the later phrase “understand physics, express physics, practise physics”.
    That phrase is quoted again and again in the opening script and in the recruitment speech.

The three-part sentence “not… nor… but…” explicitly rules out two things the club was not:
**problem-solving drills** and **the number of extracurricular activities**.

## Slide 5 · 會費收集說明 (Membership dues collection)

> **會費收集說明 / Membership Fee Collection Instructions**
>
> 為了更好地支持本學期及以後的物理社團活動的開展，
> 本學期將設置社團會費**自願繳納機制**。
>
> 特別說明的是：**本次會費繳納完全遵循自願原則。**
> 繳納會費並不是加入社團的前提，不繳納也不會影響同學參與社團日常活動。

> In order to better support the development of the physics club activities this term,
> a voluntary membership fee payment system will be implemented this term.
>
> It should be noted specifically: This fee payment is entirely based on a
> **voluntary principle**. Paying the fee is not a prerequisite for joining the club;
> failure to pay will not affect students' participation in the club's daily activities.

!!! note "The voluntary principle stated three times"
    This slide defines the same thing three times over — “**自願繳納機制**”, “**完全遵循自願原則**”,
    “**不繳納也不會影響**” — with the wording growing stronger each time.
    It is the most cautious page in the surviving material on the question of dues.

The specific uses and the principles for spending them are set out in [Notes on how dues were used](../../about/sources/index.md#dues-usage "物理社会费使用说明.docx");
the system design is in [Membership dues and contribution credits](../../system/governance/fees.md).

## Slide 7 · 關於招新 (About recruitment)

> **關於招新 / Regarding the recruitment of new members**
>
> 新學期，物理社團正式開啟招新。如果你對物理有興趣，如果你喜歡思考、喜歡探索、
> 喜歡動手，如果你曾經被某一個實驗、某一個現象、某一個問題點燃過好奇心，
> 那麼歡迎你加入我們。
>
> **你不需要一開始就「很厲害」，也不需要已經學會很多超綱知識。**
> 真正重要的是，你願不願意保持好奇，願不願意去問一個「為什麼」，
> 願不願意在看似普通的世界裏，發現那些不普通的規律。

**English rendering.**

Regarding the recruitment of new members.

In the new term, the physics club formally opens recruitment. If you are interested in physics, if you like to think,
like to explore, like to work with your hands, if some experiment, some phenomenon, some question has ever
caught your curiosity, then you are welcome to join us.

**You do not have to be “very good” from the start, and you do not need to have learned a great deal beyond the syllabus.**
What really matters is whether you are willing to stay curious, willing to ask “why”, willing to find the uncommon
laws in a world that looks ordinary.

!!! note "How the threshold for recruitment is put"
    This slide explicitly **removes the knowledge threshold** (“you do not have to be very good”, “no knowledge beyond the syllabus”),
    and turns the test into a matter of **willingness** (curiosity / asking further / noticing).
    The wording answers directly to the club's purpose on slide 2.

## Slide 9 · 自媒體部門說明 (The social media department)

> **招募方向**
> 拍攝、剪輯、策劃、文案、封面設計、運營
>
> **運轉方式**
> **盈利前由 Sink 每月注入 1000 元專項經費**
>
> **部門目標**
> 讓熱愛被看見，讓內容產生價值，讓社團擁有自己的未來
>
> **適用場景**
> 公眾號推文 / 招新發佈 / 集會宣講配套文案
>
> **讓熱愛被看見 · 讓內容產生價值 · 讓社團擁有自己的未來**

**English rendering.**

**What we are looking for** — filming, editing, planning, copywriting, cover design, operations.

**How it runs** — **before it makes a profit, Sink puts in 1000 yuan a month as dedicated funding**.

**What the department aims at** — let what we love be seen, let the content have value, let the club have a future of its own.

**Where it applies** — the club's WeChat account posts / recruitment announcements / supporting copy for assembly talks.

**Let what we love be seen · let the content have value · let the club have a future of its own.**

> What we value more is: whether you are willing to do something seriously,
> whether you are willing to take on responsibilities within the team,
> and whether you are willing to turn your interests into real abilities.

!!! note "What this slide is"
    This is the social media department's **founding statement**, with four elements:
    what it recruits for, how it runs, what the department aims at, and where it applies.
    The line “before it turns a profit, Sink puts in a dedicated 1000 yuan a month” was **announced publicly at the assembly**,
    and is the only record of a funding source in the surviving material.
