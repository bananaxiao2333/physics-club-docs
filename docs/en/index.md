## Beyond physics, there was us — exploring freely

The ASJ Yingdong Physics Club set out to create a platform:
**a place for students to understand, express, and practise physics in their own way.**

What is collected here is what it left behind — speech scripts, event plans,
designs, and photographs. The club no longer exists, but the things people
worked on seriously are worth remembering.

## What this site is

This site is a **documentary reconstruction**. Every factual claim is traceable
to a surviving source file in the club's materials archive. Where sources
disagree, the disagreement is preserved rather than resolved.

!!! warning "Editorial principles"
    - Nothing unknown is invented. **The club's end date and reason cannot be
      confirmed from the materials**, and are not supplied.
    - When materials conflict — on timing, competition format, group size, or
      award rates — **all versions are shown side by side**.
    - **No competition results are published.** The three event forms survive
      as blank templates only; there is no record of who won.
    - Drafts found in an expired design folder are archived **as drafts**,
      never asserted to be the version actually issued.
