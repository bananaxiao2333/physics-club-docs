## There was no closing ceremony

There was no closing ceremony, no final event, and no formal farewell script.
It was simply that from that day on, assemblies were no longer scheduled, competitions no longer took
entries, the purchasing list stopped gaining rows, and club badges stopped reaching new members' hands.

## We chose to record it as it was

This site does not intend to turn the suspension into a drama. Something quite ordinary happened:
a group of people made some things together over a stretch of time, and then that stretch of time ended.

In the 27 April version of the assembly slides, the time of the paper plane challenge had just been
changed to 15:20–16:20; the 21 April recruitment questionnaire was still asking students “can you accept
a 50-yuan club fee”; in the 12 May slides the four groups had just been divided, and the demo video for
the merit system had just been finished. All of it is still here, nothing cleared away.

This site is itself one of the things left behind: all the original files sorted, archived and made
public, so that this period can be searched rather than scattered in a folder no one opens again.
