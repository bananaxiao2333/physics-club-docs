# The First Assembly and Recruitment of the Term

<p class="memorial-kicker">2026.04.21 · ASSEMBLY MINUTES</p>

## 2 · The Paper Plane Flight Festival

The paper plane activity of the science and technology festival was formally announced.
The spoken script deliberately corrected a common misunderstanding:

> It is not a game of “fold it anyhow and throw it”. It can itself become a very good physics inquiry activity.

What the script pointed to was this question: **where the initial velocity of the flight comes from,
how energy is transformed during the flight, and how factors such as wing shape, the position of the
centre of mass and air resistance affect how it performs.**

> What we hope everyone finally sees is not only “who flew the furthest”,
> but: whose design had the most thought in it, who solved the problem best with physical thinking,
> and who could give a simple paper plane a genuine touch of engineering.

→ The activity plan is in the [Flight Project](../flight/index.md) and the [Run of Show](../flight/runbook.md)

## 3 · Competition results

Last year **28** students entered the physics competition and **100% won awards**,
with a gold award rate of **90%** (rounded).

The position was restated: setting out the results is not pressure, it shows that this club can be
“good fun” and “properly serious” at the same time.

→ See [Physics Competition · A 100% Award Rate](ipc-2026.md)

## 4 · Recruitment

> You do not need to be “very good” from the start, and you do not need to have learned a lot of
> material beyond the syllabus. What really matters is whether you are willing to stay curious,
> whether you are willing to ask a “why”, and whether you are willing to find the uncommon rules
> in a world that looks ordinary.

During recruitment a “Physics Club Recruitment Interest Questionnaire” was also handed out,
asking about the acceptable range for club dues (50 / 100 / 200 yuan or other),
skills and role preferences (editing, photography, both, none for now),
and whether students were willing to take part in content production for pay.

## 6 · Membership dues

Paying dues this time was **entirely voluntary**; paying was not a condition of joining the club,
and not paying did not affect a student's taking part in the club's ordinary activities.

The rules on use, oversight and accounts are in [Membership Dues and Contribution Credits](../system/governance/fees.md).
