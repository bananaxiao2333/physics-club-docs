## How we did it

Not “handing out answers”, but **taking problems apart, building models, and training a real way of
thinking in physics**. The club's two lines were pursued in parallel:

1. **Make physics interesting, tangible and open to participation** — experiments, activities, challenges
2. **Let students who want to go deeper go further** — competition problem walkthroughs and thinking training

The recruitment script puts this line this way:

> Here it is not about “handing out answers”, but about taking problems apart, building models and
> training a real way of thinking in physics, so that students can move from “able to solve problems”
> to “able to think”.

## A position in one line

Competition results are not meant to create pressure. We include this part
not to say “join the club and you have to compete”,
but to say: **this club can be good fun and properly serious at the same time.**
