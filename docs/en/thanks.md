## An afterword

### Not every story has a solemn full stop

Some things stop, and what is left is a few documents, some photographs,
and ideas there was not time to finish explaining.

This site puts them together. It gives the people who took part a place they
can come back to, and tells anyone passing later that
**here there was once a group of people who thought seriously, tried,
and made things together.**

"Freedom Was Here" is the name of this memorial. It commemorates
**the freedom to ask**, the warmth of exploring, and the real connections
that happened between people.

This page is dedicated to every member, every teacher, every participant,
and to the help that went unrecorded.

## Editorial notes

### Basis

This site is compiled from the club's surviving materials, and **every fact is
traceable to an original file**.

The complete file-by-file inventory — including which document establishes
which fact — is on the [Sources](sources.md) page.

### Photographs and visual material

Photographs come from `微信assets`; paper-plane images, the venue map and the
poster come from the same folder or were extracted from the presentations.
Images are optimised for web viewing.
**No stock photography is used in place of club activity.**

### Fact and memorial prose

!!! warning "The boundaries of this site"
    - The club's ended state rests on the **commissioning brief** for this
      memorial. The materials **cannot confirm** the founding date, the end
      date, the reason for ending, or the flight festival's final results.
      **This site does not supply them.**
    - Recruitment, fees and system designs found in plans are treated as
      **historical material**. This site offers no registration, no payment
      collection, and no club account.
    - **Lyrical prose on this site was written for the memorial** and is not
      presented as anyone's original words.
