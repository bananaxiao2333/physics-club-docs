## A note written afterwards

### Not every story gets a solemn full stop

Some things stop, and what they leave behind is a few files, some photographs, and ideas there was no time to finish saying.

This website puts them together in one place. It gives those who once took part a place they can come back to, and lets those who pass by later know: **there was once a group of people here who thought seriously, tried, and did things together.**

“Freedom Was Here” is the name of this memorial. It commemorates **the freedom to ask**, **the appetite to explore**, and the connections that genuinely happened between people.

This page is offered to every member, teacher and participant, and to the help that was never recorded.
