## Editorial notes

### What the content rests on

This site is compiled from the material above. **Lyrical passages on its pages were written for the memorial site** and are not the words of the historical people involved; wherever an assembly script is quoted, the source material is cited.

### Photographs and visual material

The photographs come from [Original on-site photographs](index.md#photo-assets); the paper plane illustrations, venue map and poster come from the same material directory or were extracted from the presentations.
Images were optimised in size and format for web viewing. **No image from the internet was passed off as club activity.**

### Conflicting statements of time between the materials { #time-conflicts }

The surviving material gives **four different versions** of the Flight Festival's timing. This site keeps them side by side and does not presume to settle what was finally carried out:

| Source | Stated time |
| --- | --- |
| 4.21 assembly, final version | 30 April ==14:00–16:30== |
| Flight Festival division of work | 30 April ==14:00–16:30== (sub-items extending to 17:30) |
| 4.27 assembly notes | changed to ==15:20–16:20== |
| Poster original | ==15:00–16:10== |
| Run-of-show document | ==all day==: morning gymnasium 08:00–12:30 + afternoon main sports field 13:30–16:00 |

### Matters that cannot be confirmed

!!! warning "What this site does not fill in"
    - **The club's end date and reason** — the material cannot confirm them, so this site does not fill them in.
    - **The Flight Festival's final results** — the surviving material contains only preparation files and blank forms (the results sheet and the sign-in sheet were both left unfilled), **with no record of any actual result**.
    - **The number of competition entrants** — the material can confirm an award rate of 100%, but **no original record of the number of entrants was found**.
