## The club's stated purpose

The only positive definition of the club's purpose in the surviving materials
comes from page 2 of the 21 April 2026 assembly deck:

> The birth of the ASJ Physics Club was not merely to solve problems,
> nor to add to the number of extracurricular activities, but to create
> **a platform for students to understand, express, and practise physics
> in their own way.**

The "not… nor… but…" construction explicitly rules out two other identities:
a problem-drilling group, and a line item on an activities list.

The opening speech script split the club's direction into two parallel tracks:

1. **Make physics interesting, tangible, and participatory** — experiments,
   events, challenges.
2. **Let those who want to go deeper go further** — competition problem
   walkthroughs and training in physical reasoning.
