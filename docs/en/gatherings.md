## Turn back to that page

Three presentation decks from spring 2026. **31 slides**, original order preserved.

!!! info "About the transcription"
    Each deck is transcribed in full in the Chinese edition, slide by slide,
    with the original traditional-Chinese wording quoted verbatim.

    Deck schedules are recorded as planned or as changed — **never as what
    actually happened**.

## Why the 12 May deck matters

| Dimension | Note |
| --- | --- |
| Date | 2026-05-12 — **the latest date in the entire archive** |
| Length | 5 slides, 3 with content |
| Unique content | The **only complete group roster** (85 members) and the only merit-system showing |
| Missing content | No schedule, no event plan, no budget detail |

Its two substantive slides both point the same way: in May the club moved from
*holding events* to *writing rules and dividing into groups*.
